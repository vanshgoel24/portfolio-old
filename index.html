03 · Process

How I work

01

Learn the business before the screens.

I sit with founders, engineers, finance and legal until I understand the rules underneath.

02

Define the system, then design the interface.

States, transitions, defaults and edge cases come first. The screens follow from them.

03

Make the money visible.

Show estimates, dates and reasons. Never change a number without saying why.

04

Ship the core, keep the door open.

Defer what adds risk, and give engineers one shared source of truth to build against.

    One last frame

    Talk, then ship.

    If you made it this far, here's a frame of snooker. My whole approach fits in one line: Talk first, then ship.

    SCORE
    0
    USERS LEFT
    15
    FRAME
    1
    Talk to a user first: pot a red.

    The rules. 15 users are the reds, 1 point each. 6 features are the colours, worth 2 to 7. Talk to a user before you ship a feature. While there are users left, a shipped feature goes back on the table. Once all 15 users are heard, ship the features in order, 2 to 7. Ship without talking first and the frame ends. A perfect frame is 147.
